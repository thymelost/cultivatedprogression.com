A Modular Architecture for Real-Food Meal Replacement

The meal replacement category claims complete nutrition and structurally cannot deliver it at current price points. The reason is architectural: human nutritional needs split into two structurally different categories, and forcing both through a single fortified product creates dose compromises that no formulation can solve. This design separates the two delivery problems into the products that can each handle them honestly — a real-food freeze-dried shake plus a small daily water-soluble pod — and specifies the system in enough detail to be built.
